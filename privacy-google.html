Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how I, PLAGUE ("we", the "Game"), developed by GargaGames, handles your data when you play the Android app.

1. Data We Collect

Anonymous game ID: the Game generates a random anonymous identifier stored on your device. We use it only to save your game progress and process purchases. It is not linked to your name or any personal account.

Game progress: your in-game stats, levels, virtual currency balance, and settings are stored on our servers.

Purchase data: when you buy virtual currency, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, location, or any sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Data

To save and restore your game progress; to process in-game purchases; and to prevent cheating and protect game integrity.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play: in-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy policy.

Supabase: we use Supabase to store game data securely.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Game data is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at vertoprah02@gmail.com.

5. Children

The Game is a casual game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the anonymous device identifier used for gameplay.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: contact us at vertoprah02@gmail.com (GargaGames).
